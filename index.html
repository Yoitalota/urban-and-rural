About Us

A Fast-Growing Construction Firm

Urban and Rural Group is a fast-growing construction firm specialized in building, civil and rehabilitation works, solar and waste management works.

Our project management techniques are advanced and practical — essential for achieving high-quality projects within the constraints of time and cost.

Our dynamic executive and technical team of construction experts operates under a comprehensive continuous learning process policy underpinning the high-level standards of the firm.
